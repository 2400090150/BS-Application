THE STORY BEHIND BS HANDLOOMS

A journey woven with tradition

India's handloom heritage is a beautiful expression of its culture, history, and craftsmanship. For generations, skilled artisans have transformed natural fibres into exquisite fabrics, creating distinctive weaves that reflect the traditions of different regions.

At BS Handlooms, we celebrate this legacy by bringing thoughtfully selected handloom textiles to people who appreciate authenticity, quality, and timeless style. From special occasions to everyday elegance, we believe every piece of fabric carries a story worth preserving.

MEET OUR FOUNDER

Bindhu Govardhanagiri

BS Handlooms was founded by Bindhu Govardhanagiri with an appreciation for India's rich textile traditions and the beauty of handwoven fabrics.

With a vision to bring traditional craftsmanship closer to modern customers, BS Handlooms celebrates the artistry, cultural significance, and enduring charm of Indian handlooms. The brand aims to make every purchase more than a fashion choice — a connection to heritage, craftsmanship, and generations of textile traditions.

WHY WE EXIST

Preserving heritage, one weave at a time

We believe handlooms are more than fabrics. They represent generations of knowledge, artistic expression, and cultural identity.

Through BS Handlooms, we aim to celebrate traditional weaving, appreciate the artisans behind every creation, and bring timeless Indian textiles into today's wardrobes.
